## Research decision and next evidence

The design document [INSTANCE_BINDING_RESEARCH.md](docs/INSTANCE_BINDING_RESEARCH.md) explains the remaining problem and the acceptance-set equivalence of exact regional recovery and direct gating. Correct cryptographic recovery alone adds no image-separation information. Strong controls now include SSCDsingle/centered, float32/int8nine-template, existing tangent/paired controls, and releasedCopyNCEdescriptor/matcher.

Spatial local-correspondence recovery and visibility-aware error/erasure recovery are now implemented negative controls. A mechanism that estimates information loss while preserving visible instance contradictions remains an unimplemented hypothesis, not a validated contribution. CopyNCEpatchmatching, fuzzyvaults, geometric fuzzyvaults and EMedit estimation withSlepian–Wolfauthentication are direct prior art; merely combining them is insufficient. A candidate must show a new recoverable region or protocol property, with held-out edits and instance negatives, at matched absolute legitimate detection and explicit record/query cost. The latest small development cohort rejectsImprint under several existing controls; it does not establish broad superiority or empiricalFPR1e-6. Future confirmation needs fresh sources and a frozen protocol.

Related-work reading scope is in reports/shared_channel_binding_20261114/related_work.md and docs/SPATIAL_WITNESS_RECOVERY.md. Geissner/Rathgeb arXiv2506.22347 explicitly analyzes variable feature-set size and error-correction thresholds; only its abstract/metadata were checked and venue is unverified. TIP2012 already combines edit estimation and distributed-source image authentication. MarkNullUSENIXSecurity2026Section8defense is inversion-regenerationLPIPS; its latent/noise association is a different measurement. IRISwaswithdrawn and is used only for idea provenance. No new attack was developed.
